The Seven of Swords represents deception, betrayal, and the need to be cautious about others' motives. This card embodies the pain of being deceived and the importance of protecting yourself from those who would take advantage. It signifies a time to be vigilant about your surroundings and trust your instincts about people's true intentions. When this card appears, it encourages you to be discerning about who you trust and protect your interests wisely.
